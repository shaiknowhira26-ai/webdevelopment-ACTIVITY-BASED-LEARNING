Play Fair and Enjoy the Fest

Follow the Rules and Respect Everyone.

General Rules

  1. All participants must carry their college ID cards.
  2. Participants must register for their chosen events.
  3. Report to the venue before the event starts.
  4. Maintain discipline throughout the festival.
  5. Respect the coordinators, judges and other participants.
  6. Do not damage college property.
  7. Follow the instructions given by event coordinators.
  8. The judges' decisions will be final for competitions.

Registration Guidelines

  • Enter correct student details in the form.
  • Select the event in which you wish to participate.
  • Check the schedule before attending an event.
  • Contact the organizers if you need assistance.
